Delete your account

How to delete your Valen account and your data, what is removed, and what is kept.

Last updated 22 September 2026

Valen is developed by Oscar Codes Ltd, registered in England & Wales, company no. 17346219.

00 The short version

Valen gives you two separate controls, and they do different things. That distinction is deliberate.

  • Delete Account removes your account and everything we hold about you on our servers. It does not touch your training history, which lives on your phone.
  • Erase All Valen Data wipes everything Valen stores on your phone. It does not delete your account.

If you want both gone, do both. If you have already uninstalled the app, email us — see section 4.

01 Delete your account, in the app

  1. Open Valen and go to the Profile tab.
  2. Tap your avatar at the top of the screen. This opens the Settings sheet.
  3. Tap Delete Account.
  4. Confirm by tapping Delete Account again in the prompt that appears.

Deletion happens immediately. There is no waiting period, no pending state, and no way to undo it. You will be signed out and the AI coach will be locked.

Your workouts stay on your phone. Deleting your account does not touch your training history, programmes or personal bests. If you no longer want the AI coach, you can delete your account and carry on using Valen exactly as before, with everything you have logged intact. To remove those too, use Erase All Valen Data — see section 3.

Kept

DataHow long, and why
A one-way hash of your email address, and the total number of coach messages the account ever sentKept indefinitely. We keep this so that deleting an account and signing up again with the same email address does not hand out a fresh free allowance. It is a scrambled, irreversible form of your email plus a single number — we cannot read your email address back out of it, and it is not linked to any other data. We rely on our legitimate interest in preventing abuse of the free tier. See section 5 if you object
Your training history, programmes and personal bestsKept on your phone, deliberately, until you erase them yourself or uninstall the app. We never held a copy and cannot delete them for you
Backup files you have exportedKept wherever you saved them. They are yours. We never receive one and cannot delete one for you
Messages you sent to the AI coachWe never stored them. They were processed by Anthropic to generate a reply and are subject to Anthropic's own retention policy, not ours — see section 8 of our Privacy Policy
Feedback you emailed usKept while we act on it, then deleted. Email us if you want it removed sooner

One technical note, for completeness: for up to about an hour after deletion, a sign-in token already issued to your device could still be accepted by our servers, because those tokens are validated without a database lookup. The app discards its own token the moment deletion succeeds, so in practice this window closes immediately. The deletion itself cannot be undone during it.

03 Erase the data on your phone

This is the separate control, and it is the only way to remove your training history — we cannot do it for you, because we never had it.

  1. Open Valen and go to the Profile tab.
  2. Tap your avatar. This opens the Settings sheet.
  3. Tap Erase All Valen Data.
  4. Confirm by tapping Erase Everything.

This wipes every workout, programme, personal best, milestone, body weight entry and setting Valen holds on the device, along with the sign-in diagnostics log and any restore safety copy. It is permanent, and we cannot recover any of it. Uninstalling the app has the same effect.

It does not delete your account, and it does not touch any backup file you have already exported.

04 If you have already uninstalled Valen

You can still have your account deleted without reinstalling. Email privacy@getvalen.app from the address you signed up with, and ask us to delete your account.

We will delete it and confirm within one month, and usually a great deal sooner. The same list in section 2 applies.

05 Your rights

Under UK and EU data protection law you can ask us to delete the personal data we hold about you, and you can object to processing we carry out on the basis of our legitimate interests — including the retained hash described in section 2. Email privacy@getvalen.app and we will respond within one month.

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office, the UK's data protection regulator.

Our full Privacy Policy sets out everything Valen collects and why.
