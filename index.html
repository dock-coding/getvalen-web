Valen is an AI strength coach that plans every session, reads your numbers, and tells you the truth about your progress — so you never stand in the gym wondering what to do.

Private by design. Your training is yours. We don't sell it, and we don't use it to train AI models.

In closed beta on Android, with iOS to follow.

—It plans the session

Open the app and today's workout is already there, built around your goal, your history and what you trained last. No blank page, no deciding.

—It reads your numbers

Every set you log feeds back in. Valen tracks your lifts, spots your personal bests, and adjusts your next targets to how you're actually moving.

—It won't flatter you

No hollow praise. If a week slipped, Valen says so — kindly — and gives you the next step. When you've earned it, you'll know it meant something.
